Privacy Policy – Shorts Automation

Last updated: November 3, 2026

About this application

Shorts Automation is a personal video automation tool used to create and publish educational short-form videos about business and entrepreneurship.

Information we process

When the account owner connects a TikTok account, the application may process TikTok account identifiers, authorization tokens, and information necessary to upload videos.

How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate the account owner and upload videos to the TikTok account they authorize.

Third-party services

The application uses TikTok's services and may use external AI, voice-generation, stock-media, and video-processing services to create content. These services have their own privacy policies.

Data storage and security

Authorization information may be stored in the account owner's private automation environment. Access should be restricted to the account owner.

Data sharing

TikTok account information is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

Data deletion and contact

To request information about stored data or its deletion, contact the application operator using the contact information provided on this website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.
